## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need to know how to fly drones?
**A:** No! AeroCommand handles all the flying automatically. You just plan the route on a map, and the drones execute the mission autonomously.

### Q: What happens if a drone loses connection?
**A:** Drones automatically return to their launch point if they lose connection. Safety is built-in.

### Q: Can I use my existing drones?
**A:** Possibly! We support standard MAVLink-compatible drones (Pixhawk, ArduPilot, PX4). Contact us to verify compatibility.

### Q: What about weather?
**A:** The system monitors weather conditions and won't launch missions in unsafe conditions (high winds, rain, etc.).

### Q: How accurate is the AI detection?
**A:** Our AI achieves 95%+ accuracy for livestock and people detection in typical farm conditions. Accuracy improves over time as the system learns your farm.

### Q: Do I need good internet?
**A:** Basic internet is needed for cloud features, but the edge system can operate locally with limited connectivity. Data syncs when connection is available.

### Q: What about privacy?
**A:** You own your data. We don't share or sell farm data. All video and telemetry stays within your organization.

### Q: Can multiple people access the system?
**A:** Yes! Add family members, employees, or consultants with customizable access levels.

### Q: What kind of support do you provide?
**A:** Email support is included in all plans. Professional and Enterprise plans include phone support. We also have extensive video tutorials and documentation.

### Q: What's the warranty on drones?
**A:** Drone hardware comes with manufacturer warranty (typically 1 year). We offer extended warranty and insurance options.
